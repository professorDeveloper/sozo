# KAIZOKU — DESIGN SYSTEM DIRECTION PROPOSALS

> **Status:** Open for User Direction Selection  
> **Requirement:** In accordance with Phase 1C, the visual direction must be selected before implementing full UI components.

---

## DIRECTION 1: "SHIN KAIZOKU" — CYBER-OBSIDIAN & NEON CRIMSON

* **Philosophy:** High-energy, futuristic cyberpunk pirate aesthetic. Bold, rebellious, cinematic high contrast with frosted glassmorphic navigation.
* **Palette:**
  - `bgPrimary`: `#090A0F` (Obsidian Void)
  - `bgSecondary`: `#12151F` (Charcoal Surface)
  - `bgElevated`: `#1A1E2C` (Elevated Card)
  - `accentPrimary`: `#FF2A55` (Neon Crimson)
  - `accentSecondary`: `#FFB800` (Solar Amber / Ratings)
  - `accentTertiary`: `#00F5D4` (Electric Cyan / Downloads)
  - `borderSubtle`: `#1E2333`
  - `borderFocus`: `#FF2A55` (Crimson Glow Ring)
* **Visual Feel:** Edgy, vibrant, high-contrast OLED black surfaces with glowing red highlights. Ideal for anime, action cinema, and energetic streaming.
* **Interaction Dynamics:**
  - *Mobile:* Crisp crimson tap indicators with subtle haptic vibration.
  - *Desktop:* Crimson hover glow and smooth card elevation.
  - *Android TV:* High-visibility crimson focus border with ambient bloom.

## DIRECTION 2: "ABYSSAL FLEET" — DEEP MIDNIGHT & ASTRAL CYAN

* **Philosophy:** Deep-sea oceanic pirate aesthetic. Immersive deep Mariana abyss surfaces with astral cyan and phosphorescent mint accents.
* **Palette:**
  - `bgPrimary`: `#080C14` (Deep Mariana Abyss)
  - `bgSecondary`: `#0E1624` (Sub-surface Navy)
  - `bgElevated`: `#162033` (Elevated Deck)
  - `accentPrimary`: `#00D2FF` (Astral Cyan)
  - `accentSecondary`: `#00F5D4` (Phosphor Mint)
  - `accentTertiary`: `#FF8C32` (Coral Flame / Stars)
  - `borderSubtle`: `#18253B`
  - `borderFocus`: `#00D2FF` (Cyan Glow Ring)
* **Visual Feel:** Cool, sophisticated, modern sci-fi naval feel. Exceptionally restful on the eyes during prolonged late-night viewing.
* **Interaction Dynamics:**
  - *Mobile:* Fluid ocean-wave scale transitions and translucent cyan glass.
  - *Desktop:* Cool-temperature frosted sidebar with subtle teal borders.
  - *Android TV:* Luminous cyan outline visible across 10-foot distances with zero eye fatigue.

## DIRECTION 3: "GRAND LINE MASTER" — STUDIO CARBON & SOVEREIGN GOLD

* **Philosophy:** Prestige studio editorial aesthetic. Minimalist, luxurious, museum-grade typography and warm ambient sovereign gold strokes.
* **Palette:**
  - `bgPrimary`: `#0E0E10` (Matte Studio Carbon)
  - `bgSecondary`: `#18181C` (Gallery Surface)
  - `bgElevated`: `#222228` (Elevated Plinth)
  - `accentPrimary`: `#E5A93C` (Sovereign Gold)
  - `accentSecondary`: `#FF5A36` (Burnt Terracotta)
  - `accentTertiary`: `#52B788` (Sage Green)
  - `borderSubtle`: `#26262E`
  - `borderFocus`: `#E5A93C` (Gold Ingot Stroke)
* **Visual Feel:** Aristocratic, mature, premium collector feel (reminiscent of high-end Criterion or A24 streaming experiences).
* **Interaction Dynamics:**
  - *Mobile:* Subtle brass-toned touch ripples with minimalist iconography.
  - *Desktop:* High typographic density with generous editorial whitespace.
  - *Android TV:* Elegant gold hairline focus ring with warm dark backgrounds.
